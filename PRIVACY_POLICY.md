# Privacy Policy for DownGo — Fast Download Manager

**Last updated:** August 27, 2026

DownGo — Fast Download Manager ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
DownGo is designed as a desktop client application. 
- **Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- **Download Data:** All download URLs, file metadata, and file paths processed by the application remain local to your device and are never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

## 2. Third-Party Services
- **License Verification:** If you upgrade to or use Pro features, license keys may be verified via Gumroad's API. This process only sends the necessary license code to validate authenticity.
- **Browser Extension:** The optional DownGo companion browser extension for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge only intercepts download URLs locally to hand them over to the desktop app. It does not track, store, or sell your browsing history.

## 3. Network Access
DownGo requires network access solely to:
- Download the files you explicitly request.
- Verify software updates or license keys (if applicable).
- Perform user-initiated network speed tests.

## 4. Data Security
Because all your downloads and preferences are stored locally on your machine, your data benefits from your local system’s security. We do not maintain external databases containing user activity or download histories.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
